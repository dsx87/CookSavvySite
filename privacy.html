Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Who we are

CookSavvy ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by Igor Pivnyk. Questions about this policy: consul87@gmail.com.

Summary

  • We do not track you across apps or websites and do not sell your data.
  • The app works anonymously by default; signing in with Apple is optional.
  • Photos you scan are sent to our backend solely to detect ingredients, then discarded.
  • Verified subscription history is linked to your account so we can grant and restore access.
  • We use privacy-preserving analytics and crash reporting to improve the app.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Account identifier. On first launch we create a random account identifier (via Supabase) so the app can reach its backend. It is anonymous until you choose to sign in, but it remains linked to account data such as your subscription history. We attach it to subscription purchases so we can match those purchases to your account.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose to sign in, Apple provides a stable user identifier and your email address, and your existing anonymous account becomes a persistent one under that identity. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the private relay address, never your real one. We use the email address solely to identify and support your account — we do not send you marketing email, and we do not receive your Apple password. Your name is not stored.

Photos you scan

When you use camera ingredient detection, the captured image is sent to our backend AI service to identify ingredients. Images are processed transiently for that request and are not used to build a profile of you or for advertising. (Camera access is governed by the system permission prompt.)

Search queries (Smart Search)

Smart Search lets you type a natural-language request (e.g. "quick vegetarian pasta"). On devices that support on-device AI (Apple Intelligence), this text is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it. On devices without on-device AI, the text is sent to our backend, which uses a third-party AI provider (DeepSeek) to interpret it. The query is processed transiently for that request, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for advertising or to build a profile.

Usage and diagnostics

  • Analytics (TelemetryDeck). Product-interaction events (e.g. "recipe viewed") and a non-advertising device identifier used for aggregate analytics. This data is not linked to your identity or used for tracking. We do not use the IDFA.
  • Crash reports (Sentry). Crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data used to fix bugs and improve reliability. Reports can include diagnostic messages and technical context. This data is not linked to your identity or used for tracking.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple via StoreKit. After a purchase or restore, the app sends the Apple-signed transaction to our backend for verification. We retain the product identifier, original and current transaction identifiers, transaction and signing dates, StoreKit environment, expiration date, and revocation status against your account so we can grant and restore access. We do not receive your payment-card or Apple Account payment details.

How we use information

  • Provide and operate the app's features (ingredient detection, recipes, subscriptions).
  • Maintain your account and restore purchases.
  • Improve reliability and usability through aggregate analytics and crash diagnostics.

Data sharing

We share data only with processors that help us run the app: Apple (sign-in, payments), Supabase (backend/auth), TelemetryDeck (analytics), Sentry (crash reporting), and — only for Smart Search on devices without on-device AI — DeepSeek (natural-language query interpretation). We do not sell personal data and do not use it for cross-app tracking.

Data retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your server-side account and associated data, including its subscription metadata, and removes your personal data stored on the device. This does not delete purchase records retained by Apple.
  • Anonymous sessions and transient photo data are retained only as long as needed to provide the feature requested.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact consul87@gmail.com or use in-app account deletion.

Children

CookSavvy is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date.
