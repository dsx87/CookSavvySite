Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Who we are

CookSavvy ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by Igor Pivnyk. Questions about this policy: consul87@gmail.com.

Summary

  • We do not track you across apps or websites and do not sell your data.
  • The app works anonymously by default; signing in with Apple is optional.
  • Photos you scan are sent to our backend and on to a third-party AI provider solely to detect ingredients, then discarded — nothing is stored.
  • Ingredient names and recipe filters are sent to third-party recipe and AI providers to find or write recipes. Your name, email address and account identifier are never sent to any of them.
  • Some of those providers process data outside your country, including in Japan, the United States, Singapore and China. See International data transfers below.
  • Verified subscription history is linked to your account so we can grant and restore access.
  • We use privacy-preserving analytics and crash reporting to improve the app.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Account identifier. On first launch we create a random account identifier (via Supabase) so the app can reach its backend. It is anonymous until you choose to sign in, but it remains linked to account data such as your subscription history. We attach it to subscription purchases so we can match those purchases to your account.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose to sign in, Apple provides a stable user identifier and your email address, and your existing anonymous account becomes a persistent one under that identity. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the private relay address, never your real one. We use the email address solely to identify and support your account — we do not send you marketing email, and we do not receive your Apple password. We never ask Apple for your name.

Photos you scan

When you use camera ingredient detection, the captured image is sent to our backend, which forwards it to a third-party AI vision provider to identify the ingredients in it. The provider is Alibaba Cloud (DashScope), using its international endpoint in Singapore. If that request fails or returns an unusable result, the image is sent instead to Google (Gemini API). This is the only feature in CookSavvy that sends your photos anywhere. The image is used only to answer that one request — we do not store it on our servers, and it is not used to build a profile of you or for advertising. Your name, email address and account identifier are not attached to the image. (Camera access is governed by the system permission prompt. You can skip camera scanning entirely and add ingredients by hand; nothing then leaves your device for this feature.)

Ingredient searches and generated recipes

CookSavvy searches an offline recipe database on your device by default. Two CookSavvy+ features reach outside it:

  • Online recipe search. The names of the ingredients you selected are sent to our backend, which queries Spoonacular, a third-party recipe API, for matching recipes.
  • AI-generated recipes. The ingredient names you selected, together with the filters you applied, are sent to our backend, which forwards them to a third-party AI provider to write the recipe. The provider is Alibaba Cloud (DashScope) on its Singapore endpoint, falling back to OpenAI if that request fails.

Neither request includes a photo, and neither includes your name, email address or account identifier. Both are processed transiently to answer that one request. For online recipe search we cache the result for 24 hours so a repeated search does not re-query Spoonacular; that cache stores only the ingredient names searched for and is not linked to your account.

Search queries (Smart Search)

Smart Search lets you type a natural-language request (e.g. "quick vegetarian pasta"). On devices that support on-device AI (Apple Intelligence), this text is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it. On devices without on-device AI, the text is sent to our backend, which forwards it to DeepSeek, a third-party AI provider based in China, to interpret it. There is no fallback provider for this feature. The query is processed transiently for that request, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for advertising or to build a profile. If you would rather this text never left your device, use a device with Apple Intelligence.

Usage and diagnostics

  • Analytics (TelemetryDeck). Product-interaction events (e.g. "recipe viewed") and a non-advertising device identifier used for aggregate analytics. This data is not linked to your identity or used for tracking. We do not use the IDFA.
  • Crash reports (Sentry). Crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data used to fix bugs and improve reliability. Reports can include diagnostic messages and technical context. This data is not linked to your identity or used for tracking.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple via StoreKit. After a purchase or restore, the app sends the Apple-signed transaction to our backend for verification. We retain the product identifier, original and current transaction identifiers, transaction and signing dates, StoreKit environment, expiration date, and revocation status against your account so we can grant and restore access. We do not receive your payment-card or Apple Account payment details.

How we use information

  • Provide and operate the app's features (ingredient detection, recipes, subscriptions).
  • Maintain your account and restore purchases.
  • Improve reliability and usability through aggregate analytics and crash diagnostics.

Data sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, and only the minimum each one needs to answer a single request. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for cross-app tracking. Those providers are: Apple (sign-in, payments), Supabase (backend, database and authentication), Alibaba Cloud (DashScope) (primary AI provider for ingredient detection and recipe generation), Google (Gemini API) (fallback for ingredient detection), OpenAI (fallback for recipe generation), DeepSeek (Smart Search query interpretation, on devices without on-device AI), Spoonacular (online recipe search), TelemetryDeck (analytics), and Sentry (crash reporting). Your account identifier reaches our own backend with every request — it is how subscription access and free-tier allowances are enforced — but it is not forwarded to the AI, recipe or analytics providers above. They receive only the photo, ingredient names, filters or query text needed to answer that request.

International data transfers

CookSavvy's service providers are spread across several countries, so depending on which features you use, the data described above may be processed in Japan (our backend), the United States, Singapore (Alibaba Cloud's international DashScope endpoint), China (DeepSeek — Smart Search only, and only on devices without on-device AI) and the European Union. Some of these countries may not offer the same level of data protection as your own. We limit what crosses a border to the minimum needed to answer each request: your name, email address and account identifier are never included in those onward requests, and we rely on our providers' standard contractual terms for these transfers. You can avoid two of these transfers entirely: add ingredients by hand instead of scanning, and use Smart Search on a device with Apple Intelligence, where the query never leaves your device.

Data retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your server-side account and associated data, including its subscription metadata, and removes your personal data stored on the device. This does not delete purchase records retained by Apple, and it does not cancel an active CookSavvy+ subscription — subscriptions are billed by Apple and continue until you cancel them yourself in Settings → App Store → Subscriptions.
  • Photos, recipe filters and search queries are not stored on our servers. They exist only for as long as the request that uses them, and are discarded when it completes. We do keep a per-account counter of which endpoint was called and when, so free-tier allowances and subscription access can be enforced; it contains no request content. For online recipe search only, we cache the result for 24 hours so a repeated search does not re-query Spoonacular; that cache row stores the ingredient names searched for, is not linked to your account, and is deleted automatically when it expires.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact consul87@gmail.com or use in-app account deletion.

Children

CookSavvy is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).
